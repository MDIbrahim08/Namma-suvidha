# Brag Plan — Namma Suvidha

## What is this app?

Namma Suvidha helps people find public toilet facilities from a Bengaluru BBMP inventory and an OpenStreetMap-based discovery interface, while keeping listed infrastructure separate from current usability.

## The angle

**A map pin is only a starting point.** The film makes the difference between a facility’s recorded location and its current condition visible. It shows the real Citizen interface, then clearly labels the feedback device and voice pipeline as concepts. Backend monitoring is described as a local prototype, with human review explicit.

## Hook

The official logo reveal resolves into: “Find public toilets you can actually trust.” The next beat asks the practical question: “Is it usable today?”

## Key moments

- Actual Citizen Portal capture with its map, radius controls, and facility search interface.
- A single Google Flow illustration of the same citizen and restroom, used only to convey a possible feedback-device interaction. The generated subtitle is covered with a correct “OPTIONAL VOICE NOTE” label.
- A clean, text-led proposed voice pipeline and a separate, accurate description of the government APIs’ local in-memory prototype.

## Outro

Official logo still with “One city. Better facilities. More dignity.”

## User flow worth showing

Citizen discovery interface → select a search radius → review municipal infrastructure fields. The capture itself contains no facility markers, scores, or invented results. The backend endpoint was queried separately and returned BBMP-sourced records; the film does not represent those records as live cleanliness or availability.

## Tone and format

- Preset: polished / cinematic
- Direction: restrained civic-tech product film, warm Bengaluru human scale, petrol-teal and ivory titles
- Format: landscape, 1920×1080, 16:9
- Main duration: 70 seconds
- Short version: 30 seconds

## Visual identity

- Deep petrol teal `#123C3A`
- Warm ivory `#F2EEE5`
- Terracotta `#D18B55`
- Dark charcoal `#29312F`
- Typeface: system Arial / Helvetica sans stack for deterministic local rendering
- Official Kannada logo reveal is used without redrawing the mark.

## Audio direction

- Voice: local Kokoro `af_nova`, English (US), speed 0.94 for main; same voice for short version.
- Music: original, low-level ambient tone bed synthesized locally; no commercial track used.
- Mix: narration forward, restrained tonal bed under speech, short fade-in and final fade-out. Generated Flow audio is muted.
- Music cue guidance: score authored for this cut; transition accents follow scene boundaries at 6, 14, 27, 41, 54, 65, and 68 seconds.
- Restraint: no audio cue may imply that a proposed device or AI workflow is already operating.

## Storyboard (main cut)

| Time | Scene | On screen | Story job / truth status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | Brand opening | Official logo reveal and trust tagline | Brand and promise |
| 6–14 | The question | One illustrative restroom / citizen shot; “Is it usable today?” | Establish the gap between mapped location and present condition |
| 14–27 | Citizen discovery | Genuine app capture; radius range and source caveat | Show current UI; no fabricated markers or ratings |
| 27–41 | Feedback panel | Same Flow character, room, and panel; red NO tap followed by mic light; generated subtitle masked | Clearly illustrative device concept; physical hardware is not deployed |
| 41–54 | Voice pipeline | Physical device → wireless → backend → speech-to-text → AI-assisted classification → structured issue | Proposed, not deployed; human review stays in control |
| 54–61 | Government monitoring | Issue lifecycle → authorised officer review and action | Backend APIs are an in-memory demo; there is no deployed Government Dashboard and test email is not delivered |
| 61–64 | Evaluator status | Explicit “not demonstrated end-to-end” disclosure | Avoid presenting an unverified evaluator workflow |
| 64–70 | Brand ending | Official logo still and final message | Resolve the story with dignity and accountability |

## 30-second structure

0–3 logo; 3–6 problem; 6–15 genuine Citizen UI; 15–20 illustrative device; 20–24 proposed pipeline; 24–27 government backend status; 27–30 official logo and final message.
